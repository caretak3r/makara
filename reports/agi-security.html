Investment Thesis: The Security Mandate

Frontier AI labs possess the "nation's most important national defense secrets" (model weights and algorithmic IP) yet currently operate at "Level 0" security. Achieving state-actor-proof resilience is **impossible without immediate, mandated US Government (USG) intervention**, specifically the designation of AI as critical infrastructure (CISA Framework). This intervention will unlock a massive, non-optional spending cycle to build security on par with military bases, creating a multi-billion dollar, high-growth industrial sector focused on defense-grade compute, physical, and data security.
